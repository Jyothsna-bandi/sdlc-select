Project Name: Restaurant Management System

Project overview
The platform supports table reservations, digital food orders, kitchen preparation tracking, and daily sales reporting for a restaurant group. Managers and restaurant staff will review workflows in short feedback cycles.

Objectives
- Improve order accuracy between service staff and the kitchen.
- Provide managers with timely sales and table utilization information.

Stakeholders: Diners, Restaurant manager, Servers, Kitchen staff, Finance team

Functional requirements
FR-01 Diners shall reserve a table by selecting a date, time, party size, and location.
FR-02 Servers can create, edit, and submit food orders to the kitchen.
FR-03 Kitchen staff shall update each order as received, preparing, or ready.
FR-04 The system must calculate a bill including taxes, discounts, and tip.
FR-05 Managers can view daily revenue and menu item sales reports.
FR-06 The system should allow managers to update menu items and mark items unavailable.

Non-functional requirements
NFR-01 Order status updates should be visible to staff within 2 seconds.
NFR-02 The system must protect diner contact details and payment data.
NFR-03 The service should support 300 concurrent staff and diners during peak periods.
NFR-04 The system shall be available during all restaurant operating hours.

Technical requirements
The platform must integrate with a payment gateway and existing point-of-sale terminals.
The application will provide a browser-based staff interface and a mobile-friendly guest interface.

Constraints
Existing point-of-sale terminals must remain operational during rollout.
Menu and service workflows may evolve after staff feedback during pilot releases.

Dependencies
Payment processing depends on the selected payment service provider.
