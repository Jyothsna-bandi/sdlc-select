Project Name: Hotel Reservation Management System

Project overview
The system provides an online reservation and front-desk workspace for a single city hotel. Guests can search room availability, make reservations, and receive confirmations. Front-desk staff manage arrivals, departures, and room status.

Objectives
- Reduce errors in room allocation and reservation handling.
- Give guests a clear booking and cancellation experience.

Stakeholders: Guests, Front-desk staff, Hotel manager, Housekeeping team, Payment provider

Functional requirements
FR-01 The system shall allow a guest to search available rooms by date, occupancy, and room type.
FR-02 Guests can create a reservation and receive a confirmation email.
FR-03 The system must allow guests to cancel a reservation before the stated cancellation deadline.
FR-04 Front-desk staff shall check guests in and out and update room status.
FR-05 Managers can generate occupancy and revenue reports for a selected date range.

Non-functional requirements
NFR-01 Search results should load within 3 seconds for 500 concurrent users.
NFR-02 Booking and payment information must be encrypted in transit and at rest.
NFR-03 The service should maintain 99.5% monthly availability.
NFR-04 The booking interface should be easy to use on mobile devices.

Technical requirements
The application shall integrate with the hotel's payment gateway and email service.
The system will use the existing property database for room inventory.

Constraints
The first release is limited to one property and must be ready before the summer season.
Reservation changes are expected to be stable after the booking rules are approved.

Assumptions
The property will provide room inventory and cancellation policies before development.
